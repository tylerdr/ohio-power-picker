## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a good electricity rate in Ohio right now?

A good rate is usually one that stays below your utility's Price to Compare for most of your contract term while keeping fees low and terms clear. The right number depends on your utility territory and timing, so compare with current territory-specific offers before enrolling.

### Should I always choose the lowest listed rate?

Not automatically. If the lowest listing has a variable structure, a short teaser period, or a high exit fee, the total cost can exceed a slightly higher fixed plan. Prioritize total contract value over the headline number.

### How often should I re-shop electricity plans?

Most Ohio households benefit from re-shopping about 45 to 60 days before a contract expires. That timing gives you room to compare fixed plans and avoid rolling into a higher month-to-month renewal rate.

## Final Takeaway

Ohio electricity shopping rewards consistency. Compare with the right benchmark, verify contract details before enrollment, and set renewal reminders so you keep control after the first term ends. Households that do these three things usually avoid the most expensive surprises in the market.
