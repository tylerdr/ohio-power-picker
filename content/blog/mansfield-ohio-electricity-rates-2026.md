# Mansfield Ohio Electricity Rates 2026: Compare Suppliers & Save

## How to Switch Electricity Suppliers in Mansfield

Switching is your right under Ohio's deregulated market. Ohio Edison handles the administrative paperwork — your power stays on throughout the process.

**Step 1: Find your current generation rate**
Pull out your Ohio Edison bill and look for the "Generation" line. That's what competitive suppliers compete against.

**Step 2: Gather account info**
You'll need your Ohio Edison account number and billing ZIP code.

**Step 3: Compare on PUCO's Apples to Apples**
The [Ohio PUCO Apples to Apples tool](https://apples2apples.ohio.gov/) lists all state-licensed suppliers with standardized pricing disclosures. It's the most reliable comparison source.

**Step 4: Choose fixed or variable**
- **Fixed-rate:** Locks your generation rate for the contract term (6–24 months). Protects against winter and summer demand spikes.
- **Variable-rate:** Floats monthly with market conditions. Can be cheaper in mild months, but unpredictable in peak seasons.

For Mansfield homeowners heating with electric or running central AC, a fixed-rate plan offers predictability during the highest-bill months.

**Step 5: Enroll online**
Most suppliers have 10-minute online enrollment. Ohio Edison processes the switch within 1–2 billing cycles.

**Step 6: Monitor your bill**
Check the first bill after switching to confirm the new supplier rate appears on the generation line.

## Common Mistakes Mansfield Residents Make

**Staying on Ohio Edison's default generation indefinitely.** The default rate isn't penalizing you with fees — it's just that competitive rates are often 10–20% lower, and inaction is a choice.

**Picking variable when you need predictability.** Mansfield winters can push monthly usage above 1,400 kWh. A variable rate that surges in January can wipe out spring savings.

**Not checking contract length.** Some plans are 12 months; others are 24. Make sure the term works for your timeline (e.g., if you're planning to move).

**Ignoring the early termination fee.** Many plans have ETFs of $50–$150. If you might switch before the term ends, compare ETF costs against projected savings.
