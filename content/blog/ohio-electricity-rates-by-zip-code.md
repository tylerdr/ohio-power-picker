## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can two homes in the same city have different supplier options?

Yes. Two addresses in one city can fall under different utility territories, and suppliers enroll by territory. That means plan availability, contract terms, and headline rates can vary even across nearby neighborhoods.

### Does my ZIP code alone determine my final rate?

Not by itself. ZIP code helps identify likely territory, but your enrolled supplier plan, term, and rate type determine your supply charge. Your utility delivery charges still follow the utility tariff for your address.

### What if a supplier website says my area is unavailable?

Use your exact service address and utility account details. Some public pages are broad pre-filters, while eligibility is finalized at enrollment. If unavailable, compare alternatives in your utility territory.
