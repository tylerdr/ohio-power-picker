# Youngstown Ohio Electricity Rates 2026: Compare Suppliers & Save

Youngstown residents are served by **Ohio Edison** — a FirstEnergy subsidiary — for electricity delivery, but Ohio's deregulated energy market means you can choose your own electricity *supplier* and lock in a better rate than the default.

This guide covers the current price to compare in Youngstown's Ohio Edison territory, which licensed suppliers serve the area, and exactly how to switch.

---

## What You Pay Right Now: Ohio Edison's Price to Compare

Ohio Edison publishes a monthly "price to compare" (PTC) — the rate you pay if you never switched suppliers. As of early 2026, Ohio Edison's residential PTC hovers around **7.5–8.5 cents per kWh** for the generation and transmission portion of your bill.

Your total bill also includes:

- **Distribution charges** — what you pay Ohio Edison to maintain lines (you can't shop this)
- **Generation/supply charge** — what you pay a supplier (this is what you shop)
- **Transmission charge** — federal grid costs (typically fixed)
- Taxes and riders

Most Youngstown households use **700–900 kWh/month**. At the PTC, that's roughly **$53–$77/month** in supply charges alone. Licensed suppliers often beat this by 1–3 cents/kWh — saving $7–$27 per month, or **$84–$324/year**.

## Best Electricity Suppliers Serving Youngstown (Ohio Edison Territory)

Ohio's Public Utilities Commission (PUCO) maintains the [Apples to Apples chart](https://energychoice.ohio.gov) — an official comparison of licensed supplier offers. Here are the plan types you'll typically find:

### Fixed-Rate Plans (Best for Budget Certainty)
Fixed-rate plans lock in your supply price for 6–24 months. Your rate doesn't change even when wholesale prices spike — which makes budgeting easier.

**Best for:** Homeowners, families on fixed incomes, anyone who wants predictable bills.

**What to look for:**
- Rate in cents/kWh (all-in supply charge)
- Contract length (6, 12, or 24 months)
- Early termination fee (aim for $0 or under $50)
- Auto-renewal terms (watch for rate hikes after rollover)

### Variable-Rate Plans (Best for Flexibility)
Variable rates float with the wholesale market. They can be *cheaper than fixed* in low-demand months but spike in winter.

**Best for:** Renters, people planning to move, those comfortable with bill fluctuation.

### Renewable / Green Energy Plans
Several Ohio-licensed suppliers offer 100% renewable plans (wind/solar RECs) at or near standard rates. If clean energy matters to your household, these are worth comparing.

## Step-by-Step: How to Switch Electricity Suppliers in Youngstown

1. **Find your account number** — it's on your Ohio Edison bill (top right, 13-digit number)
2. **Note your service address ZIP code** — Youngstown ZIPs include 44501–44515
3. **Compare rates** — use [ohioelectricityrates.com](https://ohioelectricityrates.com) or PUCO's Apples to Apples chart
4. **Enroll with your chosen supplier** — takes 5–10 minutes online or by phone
5. **Your next bill reflects the switch** — no technician visit, no outage, no hardware change
6. **Ohio Edison still delivers your electricity** — they handle all emergencies and outages

> **Important:** Switching suppliers does NOT affect who you call for outages. Ohio Edison remains your distribution utility regardless of which supplier you choose.

## Common Mistakes Youngstown Shoppers Make

### 1. Not checking for auto-renewal traps
Many fixed contracts auto-renew at a **higher variable rate** if you don't cancel before the term ends. Set a calendar reminder 60 days before your contract expires.

### 2. Choosing the lowest teaser rate without reading terms
Some suppliers advertise 5.9¢ for the first 3 months, then jump to 9.5¢ variable. Read the full contract disclosure before signing.

### 3. Ignoring the early termination fee
An ETF of $150 on a 12-month contract eliminates 1–2 years of savings. Prefer plans with ETFs under $50 or zero.

### 4. Not re-shopping every 1–2 years
The market changes. A great deal in 2024 may be average by 2026. Reshop when your contract nears expiration.

## What Happens If a Supplier Goes Out of Business?

Ohio law protects consumers: if your supplier fails, you automatically revert to Ohio Edison's standard service with no interruption. Your lights stay on. This is one of the strongest consumer protections in Ohio's deregulated market.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does switching electricity suppliers in Youngstown affect my power reliability?**
No. Ohio Edison still delivers electricity over the same lines. You only switch the entity billing you for *generating* that power.

**How long does the switch take?**
Most switches complete within 1–2 billing cycles (30–60 days). Your Ohio Edison bill will show the new supplier's name.

**Can I switch if I'm a renter?**
Yes, as long as the electric bill is in your name. Check your lease if you're unsure.

**Is there a fee to switch back to Ohio Edison default service?**
Ohio Edison doesn't charge a fee to return to standard service, though your supplier contract may include an ETF.

**Where do I file a complaint about a supplier?**
Contact PUCO at 1-800-686-PUCO (7826) or visit puco.ohio.gov.
