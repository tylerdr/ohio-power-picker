## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are green plans always more expensive in Ohio?

Not always, but many 100% renewable plans carry a premium over standard fixed offers. The difference changes by territory and season, so compare current options before enrolling.

### Does switching to a green supplier change reliability?

No. Grid reliability remains the responsibility of your local utility. A green supplier choice changes contractual supply sourcing claims, not outage restoration performance.

### Can I choose green energy with no termination fee?

Some plans do offer low or zero exit fees, but terms vary widely. Check both the energy rate and cancellation language before finalizing enrollment.
