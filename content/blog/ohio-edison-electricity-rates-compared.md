## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are Ohio Edison and FirstEnergy the same company?

Ohio Edison is a utility operating company within the broader FirstEnergy system. For shoppers, that usually means your delivery utility is Ohio Edison while supply can come from another certified supplier.

### Can municipal aggregation conflict with a private supplier plan?

It can. If your community has aggregation, your account may move based on local program rules unless you opt out. Always confirm aggregation status before private enrollment.

### What is the safest contract length for most households?

Many households prefer 12-month fixed contracts because they balance rate stability with flexibility. Longer contracts can still work if early termination terms remain reasonable.
