### If I switch suppliers, do I stop being a Duke customer?

No. Duke remains your utility for delivery and outage service. You continue receiving utility-managed delivery service while your supply line item reflects your selected supplier.

### Can supplier rates in Duke territory change by neighborhood?

Supplier eligibility is tied to utility territory and account details, so nearby addresses can still see different plan menus. Always verify offers with your exact service address.

### What contract term is most common for Duke territory shoppers?

Twelve-month fixed contracts are common because they provide a clear annual planning window and usually manageable cancellation exposure.
