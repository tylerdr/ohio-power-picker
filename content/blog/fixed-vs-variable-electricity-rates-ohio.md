## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do variable rates always become expensive?

Not always, but they can change monthly and become costly during volatile periods. The key issue is uncertainty: you trade predictability for flexibility and sometimes temporary discounts.

### Is there a penalty for leaving a variable plan?

Many variable plans have no early termination fee, but not all contracts are identical. Verify both cancellation terms and any recurring charges before enrolling.

### Can I switch from variable to fixed later?

Yes, many Ohio customers move from variable to fixed plans. Timing matters because supplier switches take effect on billing cycles, so plan ahead if rates are rising.
