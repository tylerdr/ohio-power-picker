## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Apples to Apples the same as enrolling with the utility?

No. Apples to Apples is a shopping list for competitive supplier offers. Utility default service remains separate and is typically your fallback if you do not enroll in a competitive plan.

### Why do some similar rates have different outcomes?

Rates can look similar while contract structures differ. Fees, renewal terms, and variable clauses can materially change annual cost and risk.

### Should I use only Apples to Apples to choose a plan?

Use it as a strong starting source, then verify contract details and compare outcomes using your own usage and risk preferences before enrollment.
