## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will switching cause my electricity to be shut off?

No. In Ohio's deregulated market, switching suppliers changes your generation supply provider, not physical delivery service. Your utility still manages poles, wires, and reliability.

### How long does a supplier switch usually take?

Most switches are implemented on a billing-cycle timeline rather than instantly. You may see one more cycle on your old arrangement before the new supplier appears on your bill.

### Do I need to call my utility before enrolling?

Usually no, because supplier enrollment processes notify the utility electronically. You should still confirm your utility account information is accurate during enrollment.
