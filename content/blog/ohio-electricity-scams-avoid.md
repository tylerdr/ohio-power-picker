## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I show my utility bill to a door-to-door salesperson?

It is safer not to provide your bill details to unsolicited sales representatives. Your account number can be used to initiate changes, so only share information through verified enrollment channels.

### Can a scammer really switch my supplier without clear consent?

Unauthorized enrollments can happen if account details are captured and verification controls fail. Review your bills regularly so suspicious supplier changes are caught early.

### Where can Ohio consumers report suspicious activity?

You can document the incident with your utility and file complaints through Ohio consumer protection and utility regulatory channels. Keep dates, names, and call records for faster resolution.
