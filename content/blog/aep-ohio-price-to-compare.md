## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is AEP Ohio the same as AEP Energy?

No. AEP Ohio is the regulated utility that delivers electricity and administers default supply service. AEP Energy is a competitive supplier that offers optional market plans.

### If I switch suppliers, does AEP still handle outages?

Yes. Outages and line maintenance remain with the utility regardless of which supplier you choose. Supplier choice changes your generation supply charge, not outage restoration responsibility.

### Can I return to the default rate after switching?

In most cases, yes. Ohio consumers can return to utility default supply service, but billing timing and effective dates matter, so review account terms before switching back.
