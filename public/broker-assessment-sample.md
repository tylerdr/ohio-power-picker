### Attribution and receipt requirements

Assessment receipts need engagement ID, product tag ohio-power-picker, processor
receipt ID, currency, live/test flag, collection timestamp, amount, sales tax and
refund events/timestamps. Deduplicate events and bundle allocations; unpaid invoices
and bookings excluded. October window uses America/Los_Angeles. Count collections
net of refunds, excluding sales tax/test transactions. Fees and delivery cost go
in a separate contribution ledger; this one-time assessment creates no MRR.

Future referrals are outside scope: a named partner must agree referral IDs,
attribution window, consent, accepted enrollment criteria, rejection/reversal rules,
payout schedule, settlement evidence and disputes before implementation. A PUCO
outbound click implies no commission. Use aggregate inputs only for this SOW;
consumer identifiers and private receipt evidence must stay out of source control.


## Can this evidence support an automated Ohio comparison journey?

**Decision today:** Do not build or activate an automated current-rate referral/embedded comparison from this evidence. A bounded measurement-gap assessment may still be useful if the buyer confirms that decision and accepts the limited inputs.

**Why:** The public municipal page supports a dated, program-specific reference. The archived supplier row is not current. No buyer-owned aggregate funnel, completed-enrollment evidence or settled referral reporting has been supplied. Those gaps prevent a defensible current-offer recommendation or conversion-return calculation.

| Evidence class | Safe use in this assessment | Missing evidence needed to change the decision |
|---|---|---|
| Current official municipal page, accessed October 22 | Dated source entry with program/term/eligibility boundaries | Reconfirmation for the actual delivery date and requested territory; no assumption that every visitor is eligible |
| Archived supplier row, March 15 snapshot | Demonstrate why age, variable terms and eligibility must travel with a comparison | A current authoritative offer and terms, plus permitted maintenance/reuse method |
| Aggregate journey counts | List which measurement stages exist or are missing | Buyer-supplied period, event definitions, denominators, exclusions and reconciliation method |
| Partner enrollment/payout evidence | Mark unavailable | Agreed partner reporting and attributable accepted outcomes; a click is insufficient |

**Proposed next decision:** Confirm whether the buyer wants a measurement-gap-only assessment if no baseline is available. If yes, accept that exact scope before payment. If no, stop the pilot rather than filling the report with invented conversion data.

**Buyer acceptance question:** “Can your team identify which evidence permits a decision, which evidence is stale, and which missing input would change the recommendation?”

**Boundaries:** One territory, one journey, one reporting period; proposed $500 once and eight-hour ceiling, with one factual-correction pass inside that ceiling. No software build, live rate-feed operation, consumer records, enrollment service or guaranteed result. Existing written terms and capacity requirements remain unchanged.
